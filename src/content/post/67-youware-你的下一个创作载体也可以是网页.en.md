Recently, I came across a fantastic article: *"A Late-Night Chat with Ming Chaoping: They Don’t Believe AI Coding Will Become a New Way to Create—And I’m Glad."* The protagonist built an AI coding tool that generates web pages. Actually, there are already many products like this, but the founder used to be a product manager at several big companies. His thoughts on AI and its impact on creators taught me a lot. For example:
1. How to make the same tokens create greater value?
2. How to keep a product alive? How to design a business model?
Let’s try out this awesome AI webpage generation tool, YouWare, made by this master!

## Hands-on Experience

Below, I’ll use an article *[Resume Self-Assessment Challenge: I'll Be Impressed If You Score Over 95!](https://www.codefather.cn/post/1831925888316403713)* for testing. This article gives a standard for calculating resume scores. By following its rules, we can evaluate our resume and get a final score! But reading and calculating manually isn’t very user-friendly. Let’s use YouWare to optimize this article.
1. Copy the article content.
2. Open youware.com, paste the content into the input box, and add: Develop a website based on the above content.
3. Wait a moment—it will give us a webpage.
4. In the input box on the right, ask it to optimize further, changing it to a game style.

![image.png](https://blog-1304565468.cos.ap-shanghai.myqcloud.com/typora/20250529225629.png)
5. Click Publish in the top right corner: https://www.youware.com/project/63nxqh3ity
Now you can visit this webpage, score your current resume, and click "Calculate Score." You will see:
![image.png](https://blog-1304565468.cos.ap-shanghai.myqcloud.com/typora/20250529230050.png)

## Summary
Throughout this process, I was just an ordinary Internet user—I only needed to know how to click. Although I wouldn’t pay for this kind of product, that doesn’t mean ordinary people wouldn’t. For them, it’s like magic. I believe there are non-technical users who would be amazed by its capabilities and would pay for it. This product sparked a few thoughts—let me know if you agree:
1. Good ideas become extremely valuable, because you can use these tools to quickly bring your ideas to life 💡.
2. As AI becomes more powerful and widespread, AI creators and their platforms will inevitably emerge—maybe it’s YouWare, maybe something else.
3. High-quality content will likely ride the AI wave and gain renewed popularity in a better-presented form.
