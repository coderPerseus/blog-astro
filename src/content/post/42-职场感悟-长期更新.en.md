> Below are my real insights from the workplace

- Document and consolidate knowledge: When you encounter bugs, solve complex problems, or deal with complex business logic, record them.
- Reduce information gaps: If something can be known by everyone, there's no need for private chats. Share the information with everyone, and it might help get things done better.
